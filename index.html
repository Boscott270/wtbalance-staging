Important safety information

GLP-1 medications aren't right for everyone. Serious risks can include thyroid tumors, pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney problems and low blood sugar. The risk of low blood sugar is higher if you also take insulin or a sulfonylurea. Semaglutide may worsen diabetic eye disease (retinopathy). Tirzepatide can make birth control pills less effective. Don't use GLP-1 medications if you are pregnant, or if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2. Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and constipation.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and may differ in risks, benefits and side effects from approved products. A licensed provider performs a telehealth exam to determine eligibility, and not all patients qualify. Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly are the only US manufacturers of the approved semaglutide and tirzepatide products, and they do not supply those medications for compounding.

Peptide therapies are prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy, are not FDA-approved for these uses, and the evidence for some is limited. Your provider will discuss the potential benefits and risks with you.

This site is for general information and is not medical advice. If you have a medical emergency, call 911.
