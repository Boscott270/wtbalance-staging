How it works

Medical review comes first.

No office visit is needed. A provider decides whether treatment is right for you before anything ships.

  1. Free consultation

    A 10 to 15 minute conversation about your goals, habits and what has or hasn't worked.

  2. Medical intake

    Complete a secure online health questionnaire.

  3. Provider visit

    A licensed provider reviews your history by telehealth and determines whether you qualify.

  4. Treatment and follow-up

    If prescribed, your medication ships cold-chain, and your care team stays in touch.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do I need an office visit?

No. A telehealth visit with a licensed provider is included.

What does compounded mean?

A licensed pharmacy prepares the medication for you. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved or the same as brand-name products.

How long is the program?

The weight loss program runs 10 months in three phases, with maintenance available. You can cancel anytime.
