What "compounded" really means

A compounded drug is prepared by a licensed pharmacy. It is not the same as a brand-name drug.

The short version

Compounding means a pharmacy prepares a medication rather than a drug maker mass-producing it. FDA doesn't approve compounded drugs, so it hasn't reviewed their safety, effectiveness or quality before they're sold.

Why that matters

Trials of brand-name drugs apply to those drugs. They don't automatically apply to a compounded version, which can differ in ingredients and dose. Be careful with any site that quotes brand-name results for a compounded product.

What to ask

  • Which pharmacy prepares this, and where is it licensed?
  • Who is prescribing it, and are they licensed in my state?
  • Is a brand-name option available, and how does it compare?
  • Who do I contact if I have a side effect?

If a provider can't answer these questions, keep looking.
