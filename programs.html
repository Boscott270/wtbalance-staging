Important safety information

Medical director to supply and approve full text

GLP-1 medications are not right for everyone. Serious risks can include thyroid tumors, pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney problems and low blood sugar. Don't use them if you are pregnant, or if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2. Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and constipation.
