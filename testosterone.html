Testosterone care starts with testing.

If you have symptoms, a provider checks your labs before talking about treatment.

Request my free consultation

Signs worth checking

These can have many causes, including sleep, stress, weight and medications. Low testosterone is only one possibility.

  • Low sex drive or changes in erections
  • Low energy that sleep doesn't fix
  • Loss of muscle or strength
  • Changes in mood or concentration

How we decide

  1. Free consultation about your symptoms and goals.
  2. Blood tests, generally including more than one morning testosterone test.
  3. Video visit with a licensed provider to go over results.
  4. If you qualify, a plan that explains your options, including no treatment.
  5. Follow-up labs and visits to monitor your health.

Risks to discuss

Testosterone isn't right for everyone, and it isn't meant for normal aging alone. Talk with your provider about:

  • Fertility: treatment can lower sperm count. Tell us if you want children.
  • Blood counts: it can raise red blood cell levels.
  • Blood pressure and heart health: these need monitoring.
  • Prostate health: your provider may check it during treatment.
  • Sleep apnea, acne and other side effects.

Competing?

Testosterone is prohibited in tested sport. Read this first if you race or compete under a sanctioning body.
