Low testosterone or something else?

Fatigue and low drive can have many causes. Testing helps sort them out.

Symptoms overlap

Low energy, low drive and trouble building muscle can come from low testosterone. They can also come from poor sleep, stress, depression, weight, medications or other health conditions.

Why one test isn't enough

Testosterone levels change throughout the day and from one day to the next. Doctors generally look for symptoms along with low results on more than one morning blood test.

Before you start treatment

  • Treatment can lower sperm count, so raise fertility plans first.
  • You will need follow-up blood work and visits.
  • Testosterone is prohibited in tested sport.
  • Ask what else could explain your symptoms.
