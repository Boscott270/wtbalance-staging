About Lipo-B

What is in it

  • Methionine, 50 mg/mL: an essential amino acid
  • Inositol, 50 mg/mL: a vitamin-like nutrient
  • Choline, 25 mg/mL: a nutrient involved in liver function
  • Vitamin B12, 1 mg/mL: a vitamin involved in energy production and the nervous system

Lipo-B is not a substitute for healthy eating and exercise, and it doesn't treat or prevent any disease. It is not FDA-approved.

Side effects

Possible side effects include temporary soreness at the injection site, mild flushing and upset stomach. Get medical help for rash, swelling or trouble breathing. Tell your provider about any allergies, medications or health conditions before use.
