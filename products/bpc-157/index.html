About BPC-157

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide made of 15 amino acids. It is based on a protein found in the stomach.

Most research on BPC-157 has been done in animals and in the lab. It is not FDA-approved for this use, and human research is limited. Your provider can explain what is and isn't known before you decide.

Side effects

Side effects are not well studied. Possible effects include redness, soreness or itching at the injection site. Get medical help for rash, swelling or trouble breathing. Tell your provider about any allergies, medications or health conditions before use.
