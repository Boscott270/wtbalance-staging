The basics, in plain language. Call 503-208-6507 if you don't see yours.

Common questions

Is the consultation free?

Yes. It's a 10 to 15 minute conversation with no obligation. Medical visits and treatment are priced separately.

Is the consultation a medical visit?

No. It helps you work out your next step. A licensed provider handles any medical evaluation.

Is there a membership fee?

No. You pay a monthly price for your program, with no membership fee.

Will I definitely get a prescription?

No. A licensed provider decides after reviewing your health history and, when needed, your labs.

Do I need an office visit?

No. Visits happen by telehealth. If your care calls for labs, you can upload results from the last 12 months, visit a Quest or Labcorp near you, or choose at-home collection for an added fee.

What is a compounded medication?

A medication prepared by a licensed pharmacy. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not the same as brand-name products.

How long is the weight program?

It runs 10 months across three phases, with maintenance available.

Does shipping cost extra?

No. Approved medication ships free in cold-chain packaging, usually within 5 to 7 business days.

Do you take insurance?

No. We do not submit claims to insurance.

Can I use these if I compete?

Possibly not. Testosterone and many peptides are prohibited in tested sport. Tell us your sanctioning body first.

When am I charged, and what is your refund policy?

You buy only after your consultation and provider visit. There is no membership fee. See our policies for purchase and refund terms.
