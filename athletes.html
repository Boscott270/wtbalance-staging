You are responsible for what goes in your body, even when a doctor prescribes it.

Testosterone, growth hormone-related drugs and many peptides are prohibited in sports that follow anti-doping rules. Some medical treatments need a Therapeutic Use Exemption, usually before you start.

Tell us your sanctioning body at your first call. Then check its rules, and search medications on Global DRO. If you can't get a clear answer, assume you need one before starting.
